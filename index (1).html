Retail Analytics with SQL: FreshMart Ghana

SQL · Joins · Subqueries

Problem: A retail dataset covering customers, orders, products, and returns needed to be queried for business-relevant patterns, not just correct syntax. What I did: Wrote joins, subqueries, and aggregate queries to analyze spending, loyalty tiers, and returns. Result: Found that Gold-tier customers, just 5 of 15, drove 80% of revenue, and that returns were concentrated in two products, accounting for 12.7% of total refunds.
